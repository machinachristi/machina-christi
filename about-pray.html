Pray

Pray sets the classic prayers of the Church side by side in Thai and English, phrase by phrase: the Thai, a guide to saying it, and the English beneath, with a voice that can read either one aloud. It began as a way to learn to pray in Thai.

It rests on something much older: God can be honored in every language. At Pentecost, the crowds gathered in Jerusalem from every nation marveled: “we hear them speaking in our own tongues of the mighty acts of God” (Acts 2:11). The Church has prayed that way ever since.

Every tongue

So the Our Father prayed in Thai is the very prayer Christ taught, offered in another tongue: one faith, one Lord, many voices.

The texts

The Thai prayers are the 2010 texts of Thailand’s Catholic Commission for Liturgy, the words prayed at Mass across Thailand today. O Blood and Water, which has no text from the Commission, is taken from the Thai Divine Mercy prayers. The English is the traditional wording. Each prayer names its source at the foot of its page.

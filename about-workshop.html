Workshop

The Workshop shows how Machina Christi is built: every tool that makes it and keeps it, from the browser in your hands down to the workbench where each change is made. Each tool is a column of light; tap one to see what it does here, and the columns it works with light up beside it.

It is laid out after the square before St Peter’s at night. Steps rise in the middle to a great cross of light, and around it all Bernini’s colonnade opens its arms, which he meant as the arms of the Church, held open to everyone who comes.

Reading the square

The steps
One for each tier, from “in your hands” at the top, beside the cross, down to the workbench at the foot. The lights at the left of the screen are the same tiers; tap one to see it whole.
A lit column
A tool in use today. The flame on top is its tier’s colour, and burns larger the more it holds together.
A clear column
A tool still to come, labelled with the phase of the roadmap that will light it.
Threads of gold
Two tools that work together. Choose a column and its threads light, with sparks running out to its neighbours.

Moving around

Drag
Turn the square, any way.
Pinch, or scroll
Draw near, or stand back.
Two fingers, or a right-drag
Slide the view.
Tap
A column to read it; the same column, or empty sky, to put everything back. Escape does the same from a keyboard, and every column can be reached with Tab.

Unless the Lord build the house

Every tool here is a gift that someone made and shared: free allowances, open code, patient work. The clear columns are the road ahead; each phase of the roadmap lights another.

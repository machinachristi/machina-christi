Logos

Logos is where Machina Christi learns languages, one small step at a time. It begins with Thai, the language of the prayers on Pray: its 44 consonants as flash cards, each with the word that names it, the way Thai children learn them. ก is กอ ไก่, gaw ġài: ก as in ไก่, chicken.

open the cards

The name

In Greek, the Word is Logos. Every language is one more way of hearing it, and every letter learned is one more step toward praying in another tongue.

How it is made

The order of the letters, their classes, their words and the way they are spelled in English letters all follow a Thai learner’s workbook. In that spelling a mark over a vowel gives the tone, and a dot marks a short syllable.

The voice is synthetic: a neural voice made with Azure AI Speech, which reads every letter’s name at normal speed and slowly. Before it went on the site, every name was checked by machine: Azure’s Thai speech recognition wrote down what it heard, and its pronunciation scoring compared each word with native speakers. Three names the voice misread were respelled for it and recorded again. A machine is a second opinion, not a native speaker: take the voice as a good guide, not the final word.

Your progress through the cards stays in your own browser. Nothing about it is sent anywhere.

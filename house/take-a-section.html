Take a section

Real jobs on the house, each small enough for one person. Take one, the way each family in Nehemiah’s Jerusalem took the stretch of wall by its own door (Magnifica Humanitas ¶13).

Pray

  1. Check a prayer’s Thai against the book

    For a Thai reader · about an hour, once

  2. Lend your voice to the prayers

    For a native Thai speaker, with your consent and your name if you wish · one afternoon

  3. Pray for the people who visit

    For anyone · a minute a day

Learn

  1. Put the rooms’ names and “why” lines into Thai

    For a Thai speaker · an evening

  2. Try Logos as a beginner, and say where you got stuck

    For anyone learning Thai · half an hour

Gather

  1. Confirm one meeting’s time each month

    For someone in Chiang Mai who knows the group · ten minutes a month

  2. Host the table, once

    For anyone with a kitchen or a hall · one Sunday

  3. Walk the Holy Thursday route ahead of time

    For someone in Chiang Mai · an afternoon in March

Build

  1. Try the site on your phone, and say what is hard to read or tap

    For anyone · ten minutes

  2. Write the life of one of Thailand’s own saints for Ordo

    For a careful writer, from good sources · a few evenings

  3. Draw an emblem for a new room

    For someone who draws in line · an evening

These are real jobs. Only the last step is missing: the site has no way yet for someone to say “I’ll take it”.

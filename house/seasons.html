The seasons together

The Church’s year, kept as one house: each season with one thing to do together, at home or in person.

  1. From Sunday 29 November

    Advent

    One more candle on the front door each Sunday, and the whole house turns violet. On Gaudete Sunday, 13 December, it turns rose.

  2. 16 to 24 December

    The Christmas novena

    Nine evenings of prayer before Christmas, each night at home and, once in the week, together.

  3. Friday 25 December

    Christmas

    Mass in Thai or in English, and then the table: nobody eats alone on Christmas Day.

  4. From Ash Wednesday, 10 February 2027

    Lent: Fridays at the Stations

    The Stations of the Cross together each Friday, 12 February to 19 March, and on Good Friday, 26 March.

  5. Holy Thursday, 25 March 2027

    The walk to seven churches

    After the evening Mass, a quiet walk to pray at seven churches. The invitation ›

  6. Easter Sunday, 28 March 2027

    Easter

    Alleluia. Until Pentecost the Regina Caeli takes the place of the Angelus.

  7. Pentecost, 16 May 2027

    Every tongue

    The Our Father prayed together in Thai and English, each person in the language they didn’t grow up with (Acts 2).

The “see the house” buttons use the show home’s season switch; on the real site the season would change by itself.

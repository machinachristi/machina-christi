Show home

Every idea from the 22 November review, built into a copy of the site at once. Nobody moves in: it is taken down when the pull request closes.

A builder furnishes one house completely so you can walk through it. This is that house for Machina Christi. Walk through on your phone, then mark each idea keep, change or drop in the pull request.

Anything that would need a server is pretend and says so: meetings and Mass times are marked example, not real, candles stay on this phone, and dashed boxes like this mark where a contact or a name would go.

Built so far: stage 3 of 4.

Learn

  • X14

    Logos as a hornbook

    A sheet on a wooden paddle, as children learned to read for three centuries: a cross, a sample row of letters (tap to hear them), the first lines of three prayers, and the Our Father to end. Only Thai already on the site. Linked from the top of Logos.

Gather

  • X15

    The Gather wing

    The hall, where the house meets its neighbours (Matthew 18:20), in Nehemiah’s order: pray together, meet in person, then build. Its rooms turn from dashed to solid as each is built.

  • X16

    Gather: Chiang Mai

    Mass, recovery meetings, the Rosary, Adoration and a way to serve, each with “last confirmed” (one is flagged as three months old), the next one today, and a filter. Every entry is an example, and the page never counts visits.

  • X17

    Candles for strangers

    Choose who it is for from a fixed list, light it, and be handed a stranger’s candle to pray for. Pretend: yours stays on your phone for a day; theirs is made up.

  • X18

    The Angelus bell

    At six, noon and six, Chiang Mai time, a slim banner on every page for a quarter of an hour; the bell, drawn from a real bell’s overtones, rings when you tap. The prayer in English. Ring it any time with the switch at the top of this page.

  • X19

    The seasons together

    Advent from 29 November, the Christmas novena, Christmas, Fridays in Lent at the Stations, Holy Thursday, Easter and Pentecost: one thing to do together in each. The next one is marked, and each can show the house in its colour.

  • X20

    Invitations

    The table: a meal after Sunday Mass on the first Sunday of each month (next: 6 December). The Holy Thursday walk to seven churches, 25 March 2027. Every time, place, host and contact is a blank for you to fill.

  • X21

    A pilgrim passport

    Each room you visit gets a stamp with the day, like a Camino credencial, kept only on your phone. Walk around, then open it. The Chiang Mai list never stamps.

    The workshop out back and the building site (Nehemiah 2:18): the Workshop, Take a section, Builders, the rule and what’s new.

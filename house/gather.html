Gather

The hall, where the house meets its neighbours. Love is the network that hands it on.

When Nehemiah rebuilt Jerusalem he began with the people, not the stones: he prayed, looked at the ruins, called the families together and gave each its part of the wall (Magnifica Humanitas ¶8). The hall keeps that order: pray together, meet in person, then build.

In the show home every room here is pretend: examples say so, and nothing you do leaves your phone.

For about three hundred years, children learned to read from a hornbook: one printed sheet on a wooden paddle, under a thin, clear layer of horn. It began with a cross, the “Christ-cross row” (where “criss-cross” comes from), then the alphabet, and ended with the Lord’s Prayer. This one is a sample: the first seven letters only. Every Thai word here is already on the site, in Logos and in Pray.

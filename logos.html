learning the word, in other tongues

A place to learn languages, one small step at a time. It begins with Thai: its 44 consonants, each a card to turn over, with its name read aloud.

Thai ภาษาไทย

Thai sorts its consonants into three classes, and a letter’s class helps decide the tone of every word it starts. Learn them class by class, as the workbook does, or all together.

How the cards work

Learn shows every card in the book’s order: tap a card to turn it over and hear its name. Review brings back the cards that are due: say whether you knew it, and the box system decides when you’ll see it again. Listen & pick says a name and asks which letter it was.

The box system: every card starts in the first box. Each time you know it, it moves up a box and waits longer before coming back: a day, three days, a week, two weeks. Miss it, and it goes back to the first box and comes back today. Hard cards come often; easy ones rarely.
